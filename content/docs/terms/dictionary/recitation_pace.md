### Hadr — الحدر [#hadr]

**Definition:** Reciting quickly while keeping the letters, the vowels and the rules of delivery intact.

**Purpose:** Used as a value of recitation pace, so that a recording or a teaching session is tagged by its pace and filtered by it.

### Recitation Pace — مراتب القراءة [#recitation_pace]

<!-- source: standards/terminology/concepts/recitation_pace.yml -->

| field | value |
| --- | --- |
| `code` | `recitation_pace` |
| `kind` | `classification` |
| Values | [`hadr`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#hadr), [`tadwir`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#tadwir), [`tahqiq`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#tahqiq) |
| Origin | `standard` |
| Tier | `core` |
| Version | `v0.1` |
| Vocalized | مَرَاتِب القِرَاءَة |

**Definition:** A classification of how fast a recitation is delivered while its rules are kept.

**Purpose:** Keeps the traditional paces apart from recording styles such as `murattal` and `mujawwad`.

**Related:** [`tahqiq`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#tahqiq), [`tadwir`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#tadwir), [`hadr`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_pace#hadr), [`recitation_style`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation_style#recitation_style), [`tartil`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation#tartil), [`recitation`](/docs/terms/dictionary/recitation#recitation)
